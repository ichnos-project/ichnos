# Design Proposal Template

**Authors**: Your Name (@your-github-id)

**Begin Design Discussion**: YYYY-MM-DD

**Status**: provisional, accepted, implemented, deferred, rejected, withdrawn or replaced

## Summary

What changes, in a few sentences.

## Background

The problem, why it is worth solving, and links to the issue and any earlier discussion.

## Goals and Non-Goals

What this proposal must achieve, and what is deliberately out of scope.

## Proposal

What will change, in enough detail that someone else could build it. Cover compatibility with existing configuration, reports and command behavior.

## Alternatives

The options you rejected, and why.

## Security Considerations

The untrusted input, secrets and permissions involved, and what changes for each.

## Testing

How the change will be tested.
